# Commercial recommendations

The recommendation layer is decision support, not a profit forecast. It uses only persisted and traceable marketplace evidence.

## Routes

- `Al-sat adayı`: demand and satisfaction are sufficiently strong and evidence confidence is adequate.
- `Yerel üretim adayı`: demand and recurring pain coexist in a sufficiently large stored review sample.
- `Yakından izle`: early momentum exists but the commercial case is not yet mature.
- `Şimdilik uzak dur`: satisfaction or pain signals are unfavorable despite adequate evidence.
- `Önce veri topla`: detail, review, history, coverage, or confidence is insufficient for a directional recommendation.

## Readiness

Readiness combines the opportunity score, evidence confidence, and metric coverage. It is a market-evidence measure, not a profitability measure.

## Unit economics

The operator can persist product-specific purchase cost, marketplace commission, shipping, packaging, advertising, return provision, tax provision, other variable cost, target margin, and monthly unit assumptions. The trade desk calculates contribution, net margin, return on cost, break-even price, target sale price, and monthly contribution.

A product receives `GO` only when its calculated contribution is positive and its net margin meets the operator's target. Missing costs remain incomplete, and a market recommendation never overrides a `NO-GO` unit-economics result.

## Required validation

Every actionable route includes a three-step validation plan. Al-sat candidates require delivered unit-cost quotes, post-fee margin calculation, and a small-stock pilot. Local-production candidates require pain clustering, a costed prototype, and direct user validation.

No route is presented as guaranteed demand, profit, or production feasibility. The calculations are only as accurate as the operator's cost, tax, return, and volume assumptions.
